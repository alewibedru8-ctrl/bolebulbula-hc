Aggregates once arrived a month late. Now live dashboards support planning, KPI monitoring, workforce allocation and surveillance with no weekly compile.

How data flows

eRegister app (DHIS2 Tracker, works offline on low-cost devices) → Aggregator app (maps and validates aggregate package) → National DHIS2 (dashboards, role-based access).

Challenges met

Power interruption (battery solution built by HISP Ethiopia), duplicated effort during parallel run, peak-hour incomplete registration, and some staff still needing training. Next ask from users: interoperability.

Fully EMR and daily triangulation

The health center works with a fully electronic medical record (EMR). Every day, the number of patients recorded in the EMR is triangulated with the electronic register (eRegister) for each department, so incomplete recording is found and followed up quickly instead of at month end.
